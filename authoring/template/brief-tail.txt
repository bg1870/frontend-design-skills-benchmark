
================================================================================
PART 3 — HOW TO USE THE TEMPLATE
================================================================================

The template above is the authoring method you are to follow. Use its structure and apply
its discipline to the brief in Part 1.

Two things to keep straight:

- The template's scaffolding is for you to fill in with content specific to the brief. Its
  guidance about how to write and check instructions is for you to act on, not to copy into
  your file. Do not ship the template's own explanatory prose.
- Where the template and the brief in Part 1 disagree, the brief wins. Its hard constraints
  are binding: exactly one file named SKILL.md, 150 lines maximum including frontmatter,
  self-contained, no reference files or external links, nothing that requires asking a user
  anything, no generic exhortation, and no single house style that would make every
  artifact look the same as every other one.

Write the file to SKILL.md in the current directory. Output exactly that one file.
