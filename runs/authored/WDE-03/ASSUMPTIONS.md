# Material assumptions

- **Brand:** “Northstar” is a fictional B2B product analytics platform; no existing brand assets or design system were supplied.
- **Pricing model:** Pricing is illustrative: free Explore, Scale from $300 monthly / $240 on annual billing, and custom Enterprise pricing. Usage estimates are directional rather than a binding quote.
- **Conversion path:** In the absence of application routes or backend services, conversion and legal links use pre-addressed email actions.
- **Packaging:** Seats are unlimited and Scale pricing is based on monthly tracked events; this informed both page copy and the interactive estimator.
- **Delivery:** The artifact is intentionally dependency-free and contained in `index.html`, so it can be opened directly or served by any static host.
