# Ridgeline marketing site

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. The site is self-contained and has no build step or external assets.

## Material assumptions
- Ridgeline is positioned as an all-in-one scheduling, time tracking, and payroll product for independent restaurants.
- No pricing, customer names, performance metrics, or compliance guarantees are stated because none were supplied.
- “Book a walkthrough” is the primary conversion action. Its form is an explicitly labeled local-only demonstration because no backend or destination was provided.
- Product data shown in the schedule is representative sample content, not a customer claim.
