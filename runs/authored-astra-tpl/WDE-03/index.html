Useful details

Before you decide.

What counts as a tracked user?+

A unique person who triggers at least one event in a calendar month. Anonymous and identified activity from the same person can be merged.

Can we change plans later?+

Yes. You can move between self-serve plans at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades begin at your next billing cycle.

Do you offer startup pricing?+

Eligible early-stage companies can request a reduced first-year rate through our sales team.

What happens after the trial?+

Your workspace becomes read-only until you choose a plan. We do not charge you automatically, and your trial data is retained for 30 days.
