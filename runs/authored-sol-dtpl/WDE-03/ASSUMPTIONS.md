# Material assumptions

- “Signalist” is an illustrative B2B analytics brand created for this artifact.
- Prices, plan limits, feature availability, trial terms, and contact addresses are placeholder product content and require commercial/legal review before launch.
- Annual prices are shown as monthly equivalents and represent a 20% discount; billing copy changes to “billed annually.”
- The page is delivered as a dependency-free static site (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`) and can be served from any static host.
- Google Fonts are an enhancement; system fallbacks preserve the layout if they do not load.
