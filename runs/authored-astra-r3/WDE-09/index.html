The problem
02 / 08

The warehouse changes faster than its conveyor.

Operators are trapped between labor-heavy manual sortation and disruptive, capital-intensive rebuilds.

Fixed logic meets variable demand

SKU mix, destinations and carrier cutoffs shift; mechanical lanes do not.

Exceptions consume the floor

Unread labels and routing changes trigger touches, rework and missed dispatch windows.

Automation starts with demolition

Traditional upgrades can require new conveyor, controls integration and prolonged downtime.

Core insight: keep the steel; upgrade the decision layer.Problem framing · management thesis
