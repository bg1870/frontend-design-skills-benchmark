Common questions

Before you make the switch.

Can I bring over my current team and pay rates?

Yes. Ridgeline’s onboarding flow is designed to collect employee details, roles, rates, and availability before your first schedule or payroll run.

Does staff need to download an app?

The employee experience is mobile-friendly. Your team can view schedules and availability from their phone, while a shared device can be used for clock-ins.

Can managers edit a missed punch?

Yes. Authorized managers can review and correct time entries while keeping a clear record of the change.

Is this a replacement for my accountant?

No. Ridgeline organizes scheduling, time, and payroll operations. Your accounting and tax-advisory needs depend on your business and jurisdiction.
