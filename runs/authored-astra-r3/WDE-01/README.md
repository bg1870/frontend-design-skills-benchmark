# Ridgeline marketing page

A dependency-free, single-page marketing site. Open `index.html` directly in a browser.

## Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is treated as a fictional/new brand aimed at independent, single-location restaurant owners.
- The primary conversion is a requested 20-minute product walkthrough; the local prototype intentionally does not transmit form data.
- The `$99/month per location` price is illustrative, not a verified commercial offer, and is labeled accordingly on the page.
- Product screenshots are purpose-built sample interfaces and are visibly labeled **Sample data**. Names, schedules, payroll values, and workflow details are illustrative.
- No customer claims, logos, compliance certifications, or tax-filing promises are made because none were supplied.
- Contact uses the reserved `.example` domain and should be replaced before deployment.

## Implementation

- Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; no external fonts, images, packages, or build step.
- Responsive navigation, accessible dialog/form, keyboard focus states, reduced-motion support, and semantic FAQ disclosure controls are included.
