04

Straight answers

What counts as a workspace member?

Anyone who can sign in to build, edit, or view analysis counts as a member. Service accounts and report recipients do not.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. You can upgrade at any time. Annual plan changes are prorated for the remaining term.

What happens if we exceed our event allowance?

We notify workspace owners before the limit. You can raise the allowance or move to a volume agreement; data is not silently discarded.

Is implementation included?

Guided onboarding is included with Enterprise. Explore and Operate include documentation, templates, and email support.
