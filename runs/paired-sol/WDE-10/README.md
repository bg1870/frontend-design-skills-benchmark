# Loomwork signup

A responsive, dependency-free signup page for a physiotherapy clinic scheduling product. Open `index.html` directly or serve this folder over HTTP.

## Material assumptions

- Loomwork is an approachable B2B product for independent and small-group physiotherapy clinics.
- Account creation begins with one clinic administrator; clinic-specific details follow after email verification.
- Google sign-up is represented as a prototype affordance; production OAuth and account persistence require backend integration.
- Terms, privacy, login, and home destinations are placeholders because destination URLs were not supplied.
- The schedule uses fictional abbreviated patient names to communicate the product without presenting real patient data.
