Questions

Useful answers before you decide.

Still comparing options? Write to hello@meridian.example.

What counts as an event?

An event is a tracked action sent to Meridian, such as a page view, signup or purchase. Repeated properties on the same event do not increase usage.

What happens if we exceed our limit?

We notify workspace admins before the limit is reached. Your data keeps flowing while you choose a higher plan or discuss a volume agreement.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade whenever your team needs more capacity. Monthly plans can be downgraded at the next billing date.

Is there a discount for annual billing?

Yes. Annual Team billing saves 20% compared with paying month to month. The annual amount is collected in one payment.
