The fine print, in plain English

Before you decide

What counts as an analyzed event?

An event counts when Northline processes it for a report, cohort, or alert. Duplicate and rejected events do not count.

What happens if we exceed our limit?

We notify admins at 80% and 100%. Data keeps flowing for seven days, giving you time to upgrade or reduce tracked events.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade at any time; the difference is prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.

Do you offer startup or nonprofit pricing?

Eligible early-stage companies and registered nonprofits receive 30% off for their first year. Contact sales to apply.
