Buying notes

Before the first query.

Need procurement documents or a data processing agreement? Write to sales@gauge.example.

What counts as an editor?+

Editors create models, dashboards, and alerts. Anyone who only views, filters, or receives a report is free and unlimited.

Can we change plans later?+

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately with a prorated charge. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.

Is there a trial?+

Core and Scale include a 14-day trial with sample data. No card is required until you connect a live source.

How do annual contracts work?+

Core and Scale are billed annually at the displayed discount. Control agreements can include invoicing, purchase orders, and custom terms.
