# Material assumptions

- Product: **Gauge**, a B2B analytics workspace that connects warehouse data to operational dashboards, scheduled reports, and governed metrics.
- Audience: analytics leads and operations teams at growing B2B companies; pricing is in USD and billed per workspace.
- Commercial model: monthly/annual subscriptions with usage envelopes; annual billing is the default and saves 20%.
- Conversion goal: self-serve purchase for smaller teams, sales-led evaluation for larger organizations.
- Brand direction: technical and instrument-like rather than conventional card-heavy SaaS; light theme, signal-orange accent, squared geometry.
- The page is a standalone static artifact. Interactive billing, mobile navigation, FAQ disclosure, and plan-selection feedback are implemented without a backend.
