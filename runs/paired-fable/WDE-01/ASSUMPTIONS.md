# Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is a fictional US payroll and shift-scheduling product for independent, single-location restaurants.
- The primary conversion is booking a 20-minute product walkthrough; the form is a front-end prototype and does not transmit data.
- Pricing, customer names, metrics, addresses, and testimonials are illustrative marketing content, not factual claims.
- The product includes scheduling, time clocks, tip pooling, payroll, tax filing, migration, and US-based support.
- The site is delivered as framework-free HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so it runs by opening `index.html` directly. Google Fonts require a network connection; suitable local fallbacks are provided.
