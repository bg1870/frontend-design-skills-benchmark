# Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is an early-stage product without supplied brand guidelines, customer proof, feature limits, or public pricing.
- The page therefore avoids testimonials, customer logos, performance claims, and invented prices.
- The primary conversion is an email-based sales walkthrough; `hello@ridgeline.example` is intentionally a clearly non-production address to replace before launch.
- Product screens use explicitly labeled sample data. Employee names, hours, and amounts are illustrative only.
- The product scope presented is scheduling, time tracking, manager approval, tip/break records, and payroll for independent restaurants.
- Google Fonts are loaded from its CDN; production may self-host them if required by privacy policy.
