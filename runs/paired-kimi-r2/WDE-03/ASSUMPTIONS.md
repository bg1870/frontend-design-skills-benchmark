# Material assumptions

- Foldline is a fictional B2B analytics product for revenue and operations teams.
- Pricing is presented in USD per builder seat; viewer seats are free. Prices and plan limits are product configuration for this prototype, not claims of market adoption or outcomes.
- Monthly prices are $49 (Launch) and $99 (Scale); annual billing reflects a rounded 20% discount at $39 and $79 per builder/month. Control is quote-based.
- The primary conversion route is email because no application, checkout, or CRM endpoint was provided. `.example` addresses intentionally indicate integration placeholders.
- A 14-day trial is offered as part of the invented product policy. No card is required.
- The deliverable is dependency-free static HTML/CSS/JavaScript and is intended to open directly in a browser.
