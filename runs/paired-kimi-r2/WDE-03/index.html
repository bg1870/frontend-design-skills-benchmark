Before you choose

Who counts as a builder?

A builder can connect data, define metrics, and create or edit reports. Anyone who only views and filters reports is a viewer and does not need a paid seat.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Monthly plans can change at the next billing cycle. Annual plan changes are applied to the remainder of your term.

Is there a trial?

Yes. Start with a 14-day Scale trial. No card is required, and you can choose a plan when the trial ends.

What happens if we add builders?

Monthly plans are adjusted on your next invoice. Annual plans are prorated for the remaining term.
