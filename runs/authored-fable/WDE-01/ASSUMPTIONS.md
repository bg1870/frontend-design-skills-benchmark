# Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is a fictional US-focused SaaS product for independent restaurant operators.
- The primary conversion is booking a product walkthrough; the form is an interactive front-end prototype and does not send data.
- Pricing, product metrics, customer names, testimonial, support availability, and payroll-partner language are illustrative marketing copy and require legal/commercial validation before launch.
- The site is delivered as a dependency-free single HTML file; Google Fonts enhance typography, with local fallbacks if unavailable.
- Current year is shown as 2026 to match the build context.
