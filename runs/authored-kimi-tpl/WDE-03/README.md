# Metriclane pricing page

A responsive, standalone pricing page for a fictional B2B analytics product.

## Run

Open `index.html` directly, or serve the directory with `python3 -m http.server 8000`.

## Material assumptions

- Metriclane is a product and revenue analytics platform billed primarily by monthly ingested events.
- The free, growth, and enterprise tiers target small teams, scaling companies, and governed organizations respectively.
- Annual billing is 20% below monthly billing; displayed annual prices are monthly equivalents billed annually.
- Product names, usage limits, uptime figures, and contact addresses are illustrative but internally consistent.
- This is a front-end artifact only; CTAs intentionally route to a sample email address.
