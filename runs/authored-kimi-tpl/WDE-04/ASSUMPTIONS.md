# Material assumptions

- The prototype is mobile-first, shown in a device frame on larger screens.
- Habit completion and newly-added habits are session-only; no backend is required.
- Today, streak detail, and add-habit are the required interactive surfaces.
- Sample history uses a fixed September dataset to keep the prototype deterministic.
- Design direction: **Soft product** — warm paper ground, charcoal ink, coral accent, DM Sans, generous radii, layered soft shadows, and oversized streak numerals.
