Pricing questions

What counts as an event?

An event is one tracked action sent to Lattice, such as a signup, report export, or subscription change. Replayed events and internal queries do not count twice.

What happens if we exceed our allowance?

We notify workspace owners at 80% and 100%. Analytics stays available; we will help you move to the right tier before any overage applies.

Can we start on the free plan?

Yes. Observe is free for up to 100,000 monthly events. You can upgrade in place without re-instrumenting or losing saved reports.

Do you offer startup or nonprofit pricing?

Eligible early-stage startups and nonprofits can receive plan credits. Contact sales with your organization details to check eligibility.
