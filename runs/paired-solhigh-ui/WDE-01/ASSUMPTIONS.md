# Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is an early-stage US-focused product for independent, single- or small multi-location restaurants.
- The primary conversion is booking a product walkthrough; no production booking or authentication endpoint was supplied, so the form and product-preview controls are intentionally client-side demos.
- Pricing is represented as “from $149/location/month” with processing fees disclosed; this is positioning copy, not a contractual quote.
- The sample schedule, payroll totals, restaurant, and owner testimonial are illustrative. Operational UI is visibly labeled as sample data.
- No brand system or assets were provided, so the identity, copy, palette, typographic system, and CSS-drawn mark are original defaults for this artifact.
- Google Fonts are loaded over the network with deliberate system fallbacks for offline use.
