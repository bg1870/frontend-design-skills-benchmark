Buying Meridian

Questions Worth Asking

Pricing should survive procurement. If your question is not here, the team responds within 1 business day.

Who counts as a builder?

Only people who create or edit analyses, metrics, and dashboards. Anyone who views, filters, subscribes, or comments is free.

Can we start monthly?

Yes. Monthly billing is $23 per builder. Switch to annual billing at any time to reduce the rate to $18.

What happens if we exceed query capacity?

Queries continue. We notify workspace admins at 80% and 100%, then help tune workloads or move capacity. There are no surprise overage invoices.

Do you offer a proof of concept?

Control plans include a 21-day proof of concept using one production data source, with success criteria agreed before it starts.
