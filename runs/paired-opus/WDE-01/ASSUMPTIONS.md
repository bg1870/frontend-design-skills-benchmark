# Material Assumptions

- Ridgeline is an early-stage US SaaS product for independent, single-location restaurants.
- The primary buyer is an owner-operator who personally handles scheduling and payroll.
- Pricing, product metrics, customer quote, and business names are plausible demonstration content, not verified claims.
- The primary conversion is an email-based demo/trial request because no signup backend or destination URL was supplied.
- Payroll includes tax filing, direct deposit, tip pooling, time tracking, and scheduling.
- The site is delivered as a dependency-free static artifact (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`).
- Visual direction: utility brutalist, referencing printed kitchen tickets and weekly wall schedules. The memorable gesture is the oversized, tilted live schedule board in the hero; the first screen remains marketing-density rather than app-density.
