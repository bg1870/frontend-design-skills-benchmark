# Launch assumptions

- **Product:** Nearway, a free weekly email containing one self-guided city walk.
- **Audience:** Curious city residents aged roughly 25–50 who want a low-effort reason to get outside.
- **Launch scope:** Email-first MVP; no native app, account system, payment, or route library needed for launch.
- **Launch timing:** First route goes out next month; the landing page focuses on waitlist collection.
- **Market:** Copy is intentionally city-agnostic so the first launch city can be inserted later. The sample coordinates and route tone currently imply London.
- **Brand:** Original working identity. The name, domain, social handles, and trademark availability have not been legally cleared.
- **Signup:** The included form is a front-end demo. Before launch, connect it to an email platform or server endpoint and replace the `.example` contact address.
- **Privacy:** The stated “no tracking” promise assumes no third-party analytics will be added. If analytics are introduced, revise the promise and add appropriate privacy disclosure.
- **Content:** Sample route details are illustrative rather than navigational instructions and should be replaced by a researched first route.
- **Technical delivery:** Single dependency-free HTML artifact; Google Fonts are the only network dependency.
