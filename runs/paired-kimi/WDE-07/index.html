This Saturday

Water, brick & wild fennel

4.8 km72 minMostly flat
01

Begin under the old clock

Start where the market porters once checked the hour. The clock still runs four minutes fast.

02

Follow the vanished river

A blue tile appears every hundred metres, tracing water that now moves beneath the pavement.

03

Cross at the red footbridge

Pause halfway. On clear mornings, the warehouse windows turn the whole canal gold.

04

Finish with something warm

The corner bakery saves its sesame buns for Saturday. Your route ends at the window.
