# Material assumptions

- The product is **Axisline**, a fictional B2B product-analytics platform for product and data teams.
- Pricing is event-volume based, with unlimited seats; listed prices are illustrative USD monthly rates.
- The artifact is intentionally dependency-free and can be opened directly as `index.html`; Google Fonts are the only network dependency.
- Sales and trial actions use clearly labeled `mailto:` links because no signup or CRM endpoint was provided.
- Annual billing is selected by default and represents approximately 20% savings.
- US and EU data regions, startup/nonprofit discounts, and security features are plausible product assumptions created to make the page complete.
