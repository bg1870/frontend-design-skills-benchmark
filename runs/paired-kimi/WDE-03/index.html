The fine print,
in plain English.

What counts as an event?

An event is one tracked user action sent to Axisline, such as an account created, report exported, or subscription renewed. Profile updates and data exports do not count.

What happens if we exceed our allowance?

We notify workspace owners at 80% and 100%. Overages are billed at the same per-event rate as your plan; we never stop collecting data without your direction.

Can we change plans during a contract?

Yes. Upgrade at any time and we credit the unused part of your current term. You can reduce volume at your next renewal.

Do you offer startup or nonprofit pricing?

Venture-backed startups under two years old receive 50% off Launch for one year. Registered nonprofits receive 30% off any annual plan.
