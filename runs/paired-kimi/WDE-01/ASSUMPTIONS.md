# Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is a new fictional U.S.-based SaaS brand with no existing visual identity.
- The primary buyer is an independent restaurant owner/operator managing 10–150 employees.
- The core product combines scheduling, time tracking, tip handling, payroll tax filing, and direct deposit.
- Pricing is positioned at $8 per active employee per month with no setup fee or annual contract; this is illustrative marketing pricing.
- Product metrics and the customer quotation are plausible illustrative claims for this concept and would require validation before production launch.
- Demo conversion is represented by a prefilled email because no backend, scheduling URL, legal pages, or production domain was supplied.
- The artifact is intentionally dependency-light: one self-contained HTML file, with Google Fonts as its only external dependency.
