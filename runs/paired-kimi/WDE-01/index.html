Before you switch

Can you move our existing payroll data?

Yes. Your launch specialist imports employee details, year-to-date payroll, tax setup, and accrued time off. Most restaurants run their first payroll within two weeks.

Does Ridgeline handle tipped staff?

Yes. Track cash and card tips, set custom pooling rules by role or hours worked, and include declared tips in payroll and tax reporting.

What if we have more than one location?

Managers can run each location independently while owners see combined labor, scheduling, and payroll reporting. Staff can also work across locations.

Is support available on weekends?

Yes. Restaurant hours are not bank hours. Ridgeline support is available seven days a week by chat and email, with urgent payroll help by phone.
