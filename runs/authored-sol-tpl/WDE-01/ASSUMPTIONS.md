# Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is treated as a new brand with no existing identity system or supplied assets.
- The primary conversion is a product walkthrough request rather than self-serve signup.
- No customer names, performance metrics, compliance claims, or fixed prices were provided, so none are invented.
- Dashboard values and restaurant name are explicitly labeled as sample data.
- Pricing is described as tailored because no commercial terms were supplied.
- The walkthrough form is a front-end demonstration and clearly states that it does not transmit data.
- `hello@ridgeline.example` is intentionally a placeholder contact address.
