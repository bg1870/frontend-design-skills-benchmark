03 — Details

Before you commit.

What counts as a user?+

A user is anyone who can sign in to create, edit, or view workspace content. Service accounts used only for data connections are not billed as users.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. You can upgrade at any time. Annual plan changes are prorated for the remaining term; contact us before renewal to move to a lower tier.

Do you offer a trial?+

Yes. Explore and Model include a 14-day trial with no card required. Govern begins with a scoped technical evaluation.

Is onboarding included?+

Every plan includes guided setup resources. Govern agreements include an implementation plan and a dedicated success manager.
