# Material assumptions

- Clear Signal is a fictional B2B product analytics company; naming and copy are original placeholders.
- Pricing is illustrative: Foundation $0, Growth $149 monthly or $119 per month billed annually, Enterprise custom.
- The primary buyer is a product or analytics leader who values transparent limits and governance details.
- The page uses native HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so it runs without a build step.
- The page uses system sans and monospace stacks to avoid font-loading delay and external font dependencies.
- Picsum images are stable, seeded prototype assets. Replace them with approved brand photography before launch.
- The lead form simulates submission locally and does not transmit data.
- The Phosphor icon web font is loaded from its public CDN.
