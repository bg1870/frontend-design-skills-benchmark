Questions, answered plainly

No setup fees for self-serve plans. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from workspace settings.

What counts as an event?

An event is one tracked user action sent to Clear Signal, such as a signup, report export, or completed workflow.

Can I trial Growth?

Yes. New workspaces receive 14 days of Growth features. No credit card is required.

What happens if we exceed our limit?

We notify workspace owners before the limit. Data collection continues while you choose the right next step.

Do you offer startup or nonprofit pricing?

Eligible early-stage companies and registered nonprofits can request a discount through our sales team.
