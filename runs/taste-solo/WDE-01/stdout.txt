Completed the Ridgeline single-page marketing site.

### Files
- `index.html`
- `styles.css`
- `script.js`
- `README.md`

### Included
- Responsive light/dark design
- Restaurant-focused photography
- Product, benefits, testimonial, pricing, FAQ, and CTA sections
- Mobile navigation
- Accessible demo-request modal with validation, loading, error, and success states
- Reduced-motion support
- Material assumptions documented in `README.md`

Run locally with:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open `http://localhost:8080`.
