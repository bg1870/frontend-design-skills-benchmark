04 / Questions

Before you choose.

How is a final price determined?

Your package is scoped around the plan, team size, data usage, and rollout requirements. Sales will document the included allowances and any variable charges before you sign.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Your team can revisit its package as adoption, data usage, or governance needs change. Contract timing and terms are confirmed in your agreement.

Do you offer a product evaluation?

Evaluation options depend on your use case and implementation needs. Talk with sales to identify the most useful way to assess Signal with your team.

What should we bring to a pricing call?

A rough seat count, the systems you want to connect, your expected audience, and any security or procurement requirements are enough to begin.
