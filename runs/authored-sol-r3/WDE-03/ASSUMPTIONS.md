# Material assumptions

- No existing product, brand system, routes, pricing data, or product claims were supplied. The page is therefore a standalone concept for a fictional B2B analytics product named **Signal**.
- No numeric prices are shown because approved pricing was not provided. All plans use sales-led scoping language; production owners should replace this only with verified commercial terms.
- Plan names and capability groupings are proposed information architecture, not claims about an existing service. Product and legal owners should validate them before publication.
- The sales form demonstrates client-side interaction only and does not transmit data. Connect it to the approved form endpoint and add the applicable privacy disclosure before launch.
- The implementation is dependency-free and contained in `index.html` for direct local review.
