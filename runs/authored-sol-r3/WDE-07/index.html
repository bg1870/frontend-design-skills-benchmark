01 / A quieter premise

A plan should give your attention back.

Most planners turn a day into a container to fill. Sundial starts with its natural limits. Choose what deserves protected time, leave breathing room around it, and let the rest stay out of sight until you need it.

02 / A day in Sundial

Three small rituals.
One day with a shape.

  1. 08:40

    Set the horizon

    Pick one meaningful outcome and mark the hours you want to protect.

    Ⅰ
  2. 13:15

    Return to now

    A light-touch check-in helps you adjust without rewriting the whole day.

    Ⅱ
  3. 17:30

    Close the loop

    Notice what moved, carry forward what still matters, then finish.

    Ⅲ

03 / Designed with restraint

Less system.
More sense.

Time, not tasks
See the day as a finite arc instead of an endless list.
Gentle by default
No scores, streaks, or red overdue counters competing for attention.
Finished means finished
End each day cleanly. Tomorrow can wait until tomorrow.
