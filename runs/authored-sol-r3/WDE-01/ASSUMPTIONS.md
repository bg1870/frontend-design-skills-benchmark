# Material assumptions

- No existing brand system, production copy, customer proof, pricing, legal links, or product assets were supplied. The site therefore uses an original visual direction and avoids testimonials, customer logos, quantified outcomes, integrations, compliance claims, and pricing.
- Ridgeline is positioned only around the two capabilities in the brief: shift scheduling and payroll for independent restaurant owners. Supporting language describes the workflow without asserting unsupported product specifics.
- The two product surfaces are illustrative, not representations of production software. They are persistently labeled “Sample data.” Names, dates, hours, and counts exist only to demonstrate interface behavior.
- With no signup, sales endpoint, contact address, or destination URL supplied, calls to action navigate within the single page rather than pretending to submit a lead.
- The artifact is dependency-free static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and can be opened directly in a modern browser. System fonts are used because no licensed font assets were provided.
