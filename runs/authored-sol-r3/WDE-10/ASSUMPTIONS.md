# Material assumptions

- No existing product assets, brand system, routes, pricing, or signup API were supplied. This artifact therefore uses a self-contained Loomwork wordmark, an accessible client-side signup interaction, and anchor placeholders for legal/sign-in destinations.
- The schedule is deliberately labeled “Illustrative schedule”; appointment details are generic UI examples and are not presented as customer data or evidence.
- No trial length, payment requirement, compliance status, customer claims, or outcome metrics are asserted.
- Lato Regular and Bold are bundled locally with a `system-ui` fallback; no network font dependency is required.
