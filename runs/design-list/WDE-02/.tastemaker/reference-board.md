# Reference board

Created: 2026-09-07
Mode: Operate
Design read: Dispatcher dashboard for a plumbing operations team, mode Operate, with field service clarity and restrained editorial hierarchy.
Dials: variance 4, motion 3, density 8, art direction 6

## Quality bar
- Inferred, not viewed: ServiceTitan dispatch board for practical field service density.
- Inferred, not viewed: Linear issue lists for clean row scanning and keyboard focus.
- Inferred, not viewed: airport departure boards for immediate status recognition.

## Borrow
- Palette and material: daylight operations surfaces with one dependable blue.
- Type and hierarchy: compact sentence case labels with tabular operational data.
- Layout and composition: summary first, controls second, queue as the dominant work surface.
- Motion and interaction: short state transitions only where content changes.
- Asset language: one consistent Phosphor outline icon family.

## Avoid
- Generic KPI card mosaics, decorative charts, excessive sidebar chrome, and map imagery with no routing data.

## Direction contract
- Thesis: dispatchers should identify unassigned work and act on it within seconds.
- First viewport: one horizontal summary instrument above a dense, readable queue.
- System: pale blue tinted canvas, white operational surfaces, precise borders, compact radius, Geist typography.
- Risk: ornament or excessive spacing would reduce scan speed.
