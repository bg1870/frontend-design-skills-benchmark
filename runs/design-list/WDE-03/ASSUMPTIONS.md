# Material assumptions

- Product: Signalbench, a fictional B2B product analytics platform for product, revenue, and finance teams.
- Primary conversion: begin a 14 day Growth trial. Enterprise visitors contact sales.
- Pricing metric: tracked events per month. Seats are unlimited because broad access supports the product promise.
- Plans: Foundation at $49 monthly, Growth at $149 monthly, and Enterprise with negotiated pricing.
- Annual billing: 2 months free, shown as an equivalent monthly rate of $41 and $124 after whole dollar rounding.
- Proof: no customer logos, testimonials, compliance certifications, or performance claims were supplied, so none were fabricated.
- Contact links use the reserved `.example` domain and must be replaced before launch.
- Legal content is explicitly marked as prototype copy and requires review before launch.
- Layout: classic 3 plan pricing with an enterprise last mile because a B2B analytics buyer needs both self serve clarity and procurement reassurance.
- SEO: indexable evergreen pricing page with FAQ schema.
