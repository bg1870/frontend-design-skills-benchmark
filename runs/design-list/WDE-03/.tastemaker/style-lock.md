# Signalbench style lock

## Direction
Premium and confident analytics pricing presented as a clear decision ledger.

## Color contract
- Text `#191a23`, background `#fbfbff`, surface `#f0f1fa`, primary `#6868b1`, on primary `#ffffff`, secondary `#d9dbf8`, accent `#6b6bb4`, border `#e0e0e9`.
- Text safe: text on background, text on surface, on primary on primary, background on accent.
- UI safe: surface on primary, surface on accent, primary on border, accent on border.
- Border is decorative on background and surface.

## Typography
One family: Geist. Display 48 to 72, section heading 36 to 48, body 16 to 20, utility 12 to 14. No italic styling.

## Density and spacing
4 point base with 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, and 128 point steps. Pricing cards use 32 point padding and 24 point external gaps. Section separation uses whitespace.

## Shape and depth
Radius scale 4, 8, and 16. Pricing and data surfaces use hairline borders. Shadows are reserved for the recommended plan and the core product visual.

## Motion
Intersection Observer reveals use the custom cubic bezier `.32,.72,0,1`. Motion uses opacity and transform with a reduced motion fallback.

## Assets
A geometric chart mark and code native analytics workspace. No photography or customer marks because no real customer proof was supplied.

## Do not
Do not fabricate claims, logos, quotes, or certifications. Do not use background gradients, neon glow, glass cards, or decorative analytics charts.
