Ask sales a question
What counts as a tracked event?+

A tracked event is one product or business action sent to Signalbench, such as an account created, report viewed, or order completed.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. You can change plans at any time. Upgrades apply immediately and downgrades begin at the next billing date.

What happens if we exceed our event limit?+

We notify workspace owners before the limit is reached. Data keeps flowing during a short grace period while you choose the right plan.

Is there a free trial?+

Every self serve plan includes a 14 day trial with no credit card required.

Do you offer annual billing?+

Yes. Annual billing gives you 2 months at no charge compared with monthly billing.

Can your team help with procurement and security review?+

Yes. Enterprise includes a security package, vendor forms, a data processing agreement, and guided procurement.
