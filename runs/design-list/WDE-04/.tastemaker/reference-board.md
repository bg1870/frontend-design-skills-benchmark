# Reference board

Created: 2026-09-07
Mode: Operate
Design read: mobile habit tool for individuals, in Operate mode, with a warm editorial wellness lane.
Dials: variance 5, motion 4, density 6, art direction 7

## Quality bar
- Inferred, not viewed: Apple Health for legible daily summaries and restrained chrome.
- Inferred, not viewed: Streaks for immediate habit completion.
- Inferred, not viewed: Papier planners for warm paper surfaces and humane pacing.

## Borrow
- Palette and material: softly tinted paper, dark berry ink, terracotta action.
- Type and hierarchy: compact utility copy around large, steady numerals.
- Layout: one strong daily progress field followed by tactile rows.
- Motion: quick state confirmation and spatial bottom sheet.

## Avoid
- Competitive leaderboards, neon gamification, trophy clutter, generic card grids.

## Direction contract
- Thesis: consistency can feel gentle without feeling vague.
- First viewport: date, promise, honest progress, and today’s practices.
- System: warm flat surfaces, Manrope, rounded rectangles, Phosphor line icons.
- Risk: too much softness could weaken action clarity, so the primary control stays dark and explicit.
