Questions from the owner’s office.

Is Ridgeline only for restaurants?

Ridgeline is designed around restaurant roles, rotating availability, shift changes, and weekly payroll review. Independent cafes, bars, and small hospitality groups can use the same workflow.

How long does setup take?

You can add your team, roles, and availability in one guided setup. The exact time depends on team size and how much schedule history you choose to bring over.

Does Ridgeline replace my payroll provider?

Ridgeline prepares approved hours for payroll. Provider support can vary, so confirm your current provider during setup before changing an existing process.

Do staff need a desktop computer?

No. Team members can check shifts, share availability, and request changes from a mobile browser.

What happens when someone swaps a shift?

The request stays attached to the scheduled shift. Managers see the change, coverage, and approval state in one place.

Can managers correct time cards?

Yes. Manager edits keep a clear note so the payroll review shows what changed and why.

Is there a long contract?

No. The trial does not require a card, and you can cancel anytime after choosing a paid plan.

Can I get help with my first schedule?

Yes. Trial accounts include guided setup support by email at hello@ridgelinepay.com.
