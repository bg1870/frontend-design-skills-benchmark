# Ridgeline marketing site assumptions

## Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is a new brand without an existing identity kit, customer proof, product screenshots, pricing, or verified performance metrics.
- The primary audience is an independent restaurant owner who still coordinates payroll and shifts across separate tools.
- The primary conversion is starting a 30 day free trial with no card required.
- The page is evergreen and should be indexed.
- Product interface visuals are clearly labeled sample data and avoid claims about real customers.
- Brand direction: warm, capable, practical, and inspired by the rhythm of a well run neighborhood restaurant.

## Page outline

1. Floating navigation
2. Hero with a sample weekly schedule and payroll preview
3. Problem and stakes
4. Product mechanism in alternating visual bands
5. Large tagline reveal
6. Three step workflow
7. Honest proof and risk reversal
8. FAQ
9. Final trial form
10. Legal and contact footer

## Core copy

- Headline: Run the week. Pay the team. Keep dinner moving.
- Subheadline: Ridgeline puts shift planning, time tracking, and payroll prep in one calm workspace built for independent restaurants.
- Primary CTA: Start your free trial
- Proof line: 30 days free. No card required. Cancel anytime.

## Benefits

- Build a workable week faster with availability and labor context together.
- Catch time card issues before payroll day.
- Give every teammate one clear place to see shifts and request changes.
- Keep managers aligned without a chain of texts.

## How it works

1. Add your team and their availability.
2. Build and publish the week.
3. Review hours and approve payroll.

## FAQ

Questions cover fit, setup, payroll providers, staff access, schedule changes, time tracking, contract terms, and support.

## SEO and layout

- Index recommendation: index because the offer is evergreen and matches restaurant payroll and scheduling search intent.
- Title: Ridgeline | Payroll and shift scheduling for restaurants
- Meta description: Build schedules, track hours, and prepare payroll in one practical workspace made for independent restaurant teams.
- Layout: Product Demo and Workbench. The product is understandable when visitors see the schedule and payroll flow together, so the interface carries the proof.
