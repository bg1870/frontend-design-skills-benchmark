# Reference board

Created: 2026-09-07
Mode: Persuade
Design read: Single page product marketing for independent restaurant owners, with a warm operations workbench lane.
Dials: variance 7, motion 5, density 4, art direction 7

## Quality bar
- Inferred, not viewed: Toast and 7shifts for restaurant specific product clarity.
- Inferred, not viewed: Stripe for restrained product demonstration and conversion hierarchy.
- Cultural source: a neatly marked kitchen pass, where structure feels practical rather than corporate.

## Borrow
- Palette and material: warm paper, ticket blush, service red, and a cool confirmation teal.
- Type and hierarchy: one sturdy humanist sans family with large direct statements.
- Layout and composition: a single schedule proof in the hero, followed by full width product moments.
- Motion and interaction: slow, heavy reveal and a sequential benefit statement.
- Asset language: built interface fragments using explicitly labeled sample data.

## Avoid
- Generic blue SaaS styling, fake restaurant photography, fabricated testimonials, and invented savings claims.

## Direction contract
- Thesis: the restaurant week and payday belong in one calm view.
- First viewport: left biased promise with one schedule and payroll outcome on the right.
- System: warm flat surfaces, restrained coral action, teal confirmation, Manrope, rounded workbench panels.
- Risk: too much decorative hospitality styling could make a serious operations tool feel lightweight.
