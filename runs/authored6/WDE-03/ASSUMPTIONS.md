# Material assumptions

- Northstar is a fictional B2B product analytics company; all product details, pricing, and metrics are illustrative.
- The primary buyer is a product/data leader at a growing B2B software company.
- Conversion goals are a self-serve Scale trial and an Enterprise sales conversation.
- USD is the default currency. Annual pricing is displayed as a monthly equivalent and billed yearly.
- Email links stand in for unavailable signup, authentication, and CRM backends.
- No external network dependencies are required; the page uses locally available font fallbacks and pure CSS artwork.
