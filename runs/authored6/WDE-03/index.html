What counts as an event?+

An event is one tracked customer action sent to Northstar, such as an account created, report viewed, or subscription upgraded. Data warehouse rows are not billed as events.

Can I change plans later?+

Yes. Upgrade at any time, or move to a lower plan at the end of your current billing period. Your data stays intact.

Do you offer startup pricing?+

Eligible companies under two years old can receive a Scale plan discount. Contact our team with your funding stage and team size.

Is implementation included?+

All plans include guided setup resources. Enterprise includes a named solutions architect and custom migration planning.
