# Ridgeline marketing site

A responsive, single-page concept site for a fictional restaurant payroll and scheduling product. Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Material assumptions
- Ridgeline is a fictional US-focused SaaS product for independent, single-location restaurants.
- Pricing, example business, employee names, schedule, costs, and time-saved figure are illustrative demo data—not factual claims.
- The primary conversion is a personalized product demo; the prototype handles submissions locally and sends no data.
- The page uses Google Fonts when online and falls back to Georgia/system sans-serif when offline.
- No backend, authentication, analytics, or production form endpoint was requested.
