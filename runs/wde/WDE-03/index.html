Questions, answered.

Clear terms before the contract. For deployment specifics, our team can map a plan to your environment.

Who counts as an editor?

An editor can create or change dashboards, models, and metrics. People who only view, filter, subscribe to, or export content are viewers and are unlimited on every plan.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Self-serve plans can move between Launch and Scale. Enterprise changes follow the terms of your agreement.

How does annual billing work?

Annual billing charges for ten months of service for a twelve-month term. The interface computes the effective monthly rate from each plan’s monthly source price.

Do you charge for query volume?

No usage fee is shown in this illustrative packaging. Your connected warehouse or database may have its own compute costs.

Is there a free trial?

Trial policy requires product approval before launch. This concept intentionally does not promise one.
