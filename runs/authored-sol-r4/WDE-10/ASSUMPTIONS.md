# Material assumptions

- No existing product source, brand assets, routes, or backend contract were supplied. This is a dependency-free static signup artifact with an original visual system and a non-networked success state.
- Loomwork is treated as a clinic scheduling product; no unsupported customer, security-compliance, pricing, or outcome claims are shown.
- The schedule on the right is explicitly labelled illustrative and does not represent a real clinic or live data.
- Terms, privacy, sign-in, and home links are fragment/local placeholders pending production routes.
- The form collects a minimal workspace setup set. Production submission, authentication, consent storage, and server-side validation remain integration work.
