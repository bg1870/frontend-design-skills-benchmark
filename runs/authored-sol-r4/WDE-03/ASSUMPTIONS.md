# Material assumptions

- No existing product name, brand system, validated packaging, price book, legal copy, customer proof, or submission endpoint was supplied. “Northstar” and the visual identity are proposed product fixtures and should be replaced with production brand assets.
- Numeric prices were intentionally omitted. Pricing is presented as sales-scoped because no authoritative pricing source was available. Product owners should supply and approve pricing before publishing any figures.
- Plan names, capability descriptions, and packaging are proposed information architecture, not verified product commitments. Product and sales owners must validate them.
- The contact form validates locally but does not transmit data. Connect it to the approved CRM/form endpoint and add the organization’s privacy language before launch.
- Google Fonts are loaded from their hosted stylesheet with system fallbacks. Self-host approved font files if privacy, CSP, or offline requirements demand it.
