# Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is treated as a new or unprovided brand; no existing identity, product UI, customer proof, pricing, or production assets were available in the project.
- The site describes only the capabilities stated or directly implied by the brief: payroll and shift scheduling for independent restaurants. Specific integrations, compliance claims, pricing, performance outcomes, and customer endorsements are intentionally omitted.
- All interface figures and dates are illustrative and are persistently labeled “Sample data.” They are not presented as customer or company evidence.
- “Book a walkthrough” is the primary conversion. Because no booking endpoint or form provider was supplied, the form validates locally and gives an explicit integration-needed success message rather than pretending to submit.
- The Ridgeline wordmark and mountain-line symbol are original page treatments created for this artifact, not assumed production brand assets.
- Locally stored Archivo and Roboto Mono font files were retrieved from Google Fonts for this implementation.
