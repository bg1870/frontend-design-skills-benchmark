Good to know

Before you switch.

Can we keep our current POS?

Yes. Ridgeline is designed to sit alongside common restaurant POS systems. During setup, we’ll map your locations, roles, rates, and tip workflow.

What happens to our payroll history?

Our setup team helps bring over employee details, year-to-date payroll totals, tax elections, and direct-deposit information.

Can employees swap shifts?

Yes. Employees can offer and claim shifts from mobile, while managers keep final approval and visibility into overtime.

Do you handle tax filings?

Ridgeline calculates, files, and pays federal and state payroll taxes and prepares year-end W-2s and 1099s.
