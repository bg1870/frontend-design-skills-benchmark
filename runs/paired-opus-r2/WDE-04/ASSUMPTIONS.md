# Material assumptions

- The prototype is called **Tempo** and targets people who want a calm, evidence-based daily habit check-in without social features.
- Sample habits and completion history are local demo data; no account, sync, notifications, or persistence is implied.
- The week begins on Monday. Dates and weekday labels are generated at runtime in the browser locale.
- “Streak” means consecutive completed days through today (or yesterday if today is still open).
- Visual direction: Dark instrument; one gesture: an oversized, edge-cropped streak dial; dense mobile layout; dial dominates the first screen.
- Desktop intentionally presents the mobile product in a device-sized viewport rather than inventing a separate desktop application.
