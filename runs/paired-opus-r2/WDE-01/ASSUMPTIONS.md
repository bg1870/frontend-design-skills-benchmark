# Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is an early-stage product for independent restaurant operators; no existing brand system or product screenshots were supplied.
- The site avoids customer logos, testimonials, adoption figures, outcome claims, certifications, and pricing because none were provided.
- Product capabilities are framed as intended workflow, not verified integrations or statutory filing claims.
- The schedule and timecard details are explicitly labeled sample data.
- “Book a Walkthrough” is the primary conversion goal. The static demo validates the form locally and shows a success state; production delivery needs a CRM or form endpoint.
- The visual direction uses restaurant ticket-board utility: high-visibility yellow, hard black edges, and a full-width shift rail.
