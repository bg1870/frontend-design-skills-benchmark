# Material assumptions

- The product is named **Vectorline**, a B2B product analytics workspace for product and data teams.
- Prices, limits, plan names, trial length, and support terms are proposed product copy because none were supplied; they are not represented as market-validated offers.
- Email links stand in for signup and sales routes because no backend, routing scheme, or destination URLs were provided.
- USD is the launch currency. Annual billing includes 2 months and shows its monthly average.
- No customer logos, testimonials, adoption figures, certifications, or outcome claims appear because the brief supplied no verifiable proof.
- This is a dependency-free static artifact intended to run by opening `index.html`.
