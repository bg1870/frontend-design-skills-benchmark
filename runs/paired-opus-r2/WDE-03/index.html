Before You Choose

What counts as an event?

An event is one tracked action sent to Vectorline, such as an account created, report exported, or subscription changed. Duplicate events rejected during ingestion do not count.

What happens if usage exceeds the plan?

We notify workspace admins before the limit. Data collection continues during a 7-day review window so you can reduce volume or move to a higher capacity.

Can we start from our warehouse?

Yes. Analyze and Model can query modeled customer and product data from your warehouse alongside streamed events.

Do you offer invoicing?

Annual Analyze and all Model agreements can be invoiced. Monthly Observe and Analyze subscriptions use card billing.
