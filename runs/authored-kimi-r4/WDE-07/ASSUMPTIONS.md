# Material assumptions

- The launch is a small, independent software product rather than a physical or local service.
- The product is **Morrow**, a humane daily-planning app aimed at knowledge workers who feel overwhelmed by conventional productivity tools.
- Launch timing is represented as October 2026 based on the current environment date; the primary conversion is an early-access email signup.
- Pricing is intentionally omitted while the early-access period is free.
- Brand direction: warm editorial typography, botanical green, paper cream, marigold, and coral; calm rather than high-tech.
- The signup is a front-end prototype and displays a success state locally; no email backend is connected.
- Contact and social links are placeholders pending creation of real accounts/domains.
