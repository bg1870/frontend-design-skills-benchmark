# Ridgeline design notes

## Material assumptions
- Ridgeline is an early-stage US SaaS product for independent restaurants.
- The primary conversion is a 20-minute product walkthrough; the secondary action is viewing an in-page product tour.
- Pricing and customer claims were not provided, so the page avoids invented pricing, customer logos, and hard ROI statistics.
- The product combines weekly shift scheduling, time-off/availability collection, time tracking review, and payroll preparation.

## Design plan
- **Color:** Night blue `#10273D`, tomato `#F04E37`, ticket white `#FFFDF7`, prep-table blue `#DCEBF0`, mustard `#F4BE3A`, ink `#13202B`.
- **Type:** Georgia for expressive, owner-focused headlines; Arial/Helvetica for practical operating copy and controls.
- **Layout:** left-aligned editorial content paired with a large, tactile weekly schedule in the hero. Sections alternate between operational storytelling and product UI rather than repeating a card grid.
- **Signature:** the product is presented as a digital kitchen pass—paper shift slips, payroll stamps, and a real weekly roster give it restaurant specificity.

## Review and revision
The initial idea used a cream background, high-contrast serif, and small eyebrow labels—an overused startup aesthetic. Revised to cool ticket white, navy and tomato; removed ornamental eyebrows; and made the schedule itself the visual identity. Borders now represent schedule cells, receipts, and operational divisions rather than decoration. Motion is limited to the hero roster load and interactive product tabs.
