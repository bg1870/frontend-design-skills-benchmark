# Material assumptions

- The product is a fictional B2B product analytics platform named **Northstar**.
- The primary buyer is a growing data/product organization comparing plans; prices are USD and shown before tax.
- The page is a standalone static artifact, so CTA links use anchors or email rather than a real signup flow.
- Monthly billing is the default. Annual billing is represented as its effective monthly rate with a 20% discount.
- Product claims, customer statistics, limits, and feature availability are illustrative marketing content.
- Google Fonts are used when online, with robust local sans-serif fallbacks.
