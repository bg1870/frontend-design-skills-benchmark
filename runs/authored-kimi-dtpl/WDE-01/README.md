# Ridgeline marketing site

Open `index.html` directly in a browser. It is a dependency-free, responsive single-page site.

## Material assumptions

- Ridgeline is an early-stage fictional product for independent restaurant owner-operators.
- The primary conversion is a guided demo request; the included form is intentionally a front-end simulation and clearly reports that state after submission.
- Product capabilities and displayed payroll data are illustrative, not factual customer or performance claims.
- Pricing is illustrative and labeled as such because no commercial details were supplied.
- No customer names, testimonials, compliance certifications, or unsupported savings claims are used.
- The visual direction uses a warm paper neutral, tomato-red action color, editorial serif headlines, and compact sans-serif body type to balance hospitality character with payroll utility.
