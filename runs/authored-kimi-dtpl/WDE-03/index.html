Can I try Northstar before paying?

Yes. Starter and Growth include a 14-day trial with no credit card required. You can import sample data or connect your own sources.

What counts as a tracked event?

An event is a customer action sent to Northstar, such as a signup, purchase, or feature use. We deduplicate retries automatically.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade at any time, or move to a smaller plan at the end of your billing period. Your reports and definitions stay intact.

Do you offer startup or nonprofit pricing?

Eligible early-stage startups and nonprofits can receive reduced pricing. Contact our team to discuss your organization.
