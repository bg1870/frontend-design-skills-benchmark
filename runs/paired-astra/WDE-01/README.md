# Ridgeline marketing page

A responsive, single-page marketing site for a fictional restaurant payroll and scheduling product. Open `index.html` directly in a browser.

## Material assumptions

- Ridgeline targets independent US restaurants with one or a few locations.
- The primary conversion is a sales walkthrough rather than self-serve signup.
- Ridgeline includes scheduling, time tracking, tip pooling, direct deposit, and payroll tax filing.
- Product records, names, dates, and pricing are illustrative demo content; the page labels the product preview and sample launch pricing accordingly.
- The contact form is a local prototype: it validates input and confirms receipt without sending data.
- Google Fonts enhance the typography when online; local fallbacks preserve the layout offline.

## Files

- `index.html` — page structure and content
- `styles.css` — responsive visual system
- `script.js` — mobile menu, walkthrough dialog, validation, and demo approval interaction
