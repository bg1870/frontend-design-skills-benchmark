# Material assumptions

- Northstar is a fictional B2B analytics product priced by processed warehouse rows.
- USD pricing, limits, discounts, SLA, and contact addresses are illustrative product content.
- Annual billing is the default; monthly billing is 25% higher and is reflected in the URL.
- The deliverable is a dependency-free static page, so trial and sales actions open addressed email drafts rather than claiming a live signup flow.
- System fonts are intentional to keep the page fast and reliable without external assets.
