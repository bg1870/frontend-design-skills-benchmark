Pricing, answered

What counts as a processed row?

A row counts when Northstar reads it from your warehouse to answer a query or refresh a model. Cached results, previews, and failed queries do not count.

Can we change plans mid-contract?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. You can reduce your plan at the next renewal.

Do you charge for data storage?

No. Northstar queries your warehouse directly and stores only metadata and cached results. Cache storage is included in every plan.

Is there a startup discount?

Qualified companies under two years old and $2M in funding receive 40% off Explore for one year. Contact us to verify eligibility.
