# Material assumptions

- **Product:** Afield, an independent iPhone app that creates circular walks from the user's current location.
- **Audience:** City residents who want a low-pressure reason to get outside, rather than fitness tracking.
- **Launch:** October 2026, with free early access.
- **Primary conversion:** Email sign-up for one launch notification.
- **Positioning:** Calm, private and non-competitive; no social feed, streaks, pace or calorie tracking.
- **Geography:** Initial maker story and route example are London-based; the product proposition is not limited to London.
- **Form behavior:** This static prototype validates and confirms locally. A production build needs an email-list endpoint and privacy policy.
- **Brand direction:** Mist-blue map field, dark petrol ink, coral route accent, editorial serif headlines and restrained utility sans-serif. The recurring route line is the identity device.
