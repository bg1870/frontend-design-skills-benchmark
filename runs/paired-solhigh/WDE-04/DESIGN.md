# Design assumptions

- Audience: busy adults who want a calm, low-friction daily ritual rather than a quantified-self dashboard.
- Primary job: mark today's habits complete; inspect one streak; add a habit in under 20 seconds.
- Art direction: **a pocket field log expressed through cool paper, blue pencil marks, and a continuous day-line, with a punched circular day dial as the signature device.**
- Structural metaphor: field-log entries and punch marks recur in the habit rows, progress dial, and streak calendar.
- Palette: Paper `#F4F7F8`, Ink `#132238`, Pencil `#66758A`, Rule `#D8E0E5`, Blue `#315BC7`, Leaf `#16856D`.
- Type: a purposeful rounded system sans stack, with weight and numeric scale carrying hierarchy.
- Spacing: 4px base; 8/12/16/24/32 scale. Tailored 8–16px radii; pills only for compact controls.
- Layout: single left-aligned mobile log, with a quiet desktop surround. Primary action remains reachable in the bottom navigation.
- Uniqueness check: replaced the generic stacked-card dashboard with ruled log rows and one oversized day dial; avoided gradients, floating blobs, serif-on-cream styling, and decorative badges.
