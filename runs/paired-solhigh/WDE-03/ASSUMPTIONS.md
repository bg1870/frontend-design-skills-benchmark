# Material assumptions

- Northline is a fictional B2B product analytics platform for product, revenue, and operations teams.
- Pricing is based on analyzed event volume, with unlimited viewer seats as the central differentiator.
- The three plans and listed limits are product-design content, not legal or commercial commitments.
- Trial and sales actions use `mailto:` links because no application routes, backend, or form service were supplied.
- Google Fonts are used for the editorial display face and practical UI sans; robust local fallbacks are included.
- The deliverable is a framework-free static page so it can run directly from `index.html` without setup.
