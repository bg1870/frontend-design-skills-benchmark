# Material assumptions

- **Offering:** An independently produced, undated paper planner. A physical product gives the launch a concrete, credible focus without requiring an existing portfolio or SaaS backend.
- **Brand:** **Morrow** — optimistic, calm, and connected to planning the day ahead. The custom “M” line mark is used as the logo.
- **Launch:** October 2026 (next month relative to the project date), with an initial founder price of £28.
- **Audience:** Thoughtful creative and knowledge workers who want less performative productivity and more intentional planning.
- **Positioning:** Premium-but-approachable, sustainable, small-batch stationery from Brighton, UK.
- **Primary conversion:** Email waitlist. The included implementation provides complete front-end validation and success feedback; connecting it to an email provider is intentionally left as the only production integration.
- **Visual direction:** Editorial, tactile, and quietly optimistic. Forest green, paper cream, persimmon, sage, and warm chartreuse avoid generic tech-launch aesthetics.
- **Imagery:** Product scenes are original CSS/SVG compositions, so there are no stock licenses or missing image assets.
- **Social links:** Instagram is a placeholder until an account exists. The contact email uses the assumed `morrowpaper.co` domain.
