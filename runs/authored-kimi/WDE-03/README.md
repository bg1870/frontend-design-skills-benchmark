# Tallymark pricing page

A responsive, single-page pricing experience for a fictional B2B product analytics company. Open `index.html` directly or serve the directory with any static server.

## Material assumptions
- Brand and copy are original; **Tallymark** is a fictional analytics product.
- Pricing is illustrative USD monthly pricing, with annual pricing explained in the FAQ.
- CTA links use clearly non-production `.example` email addresses and are ready to replace with real signup/contact routes.
- Google Fonts are used when online, with Georgia/Arial fallbacks for offline rendering.
- The event-volume control intentionally updates Scale pricing as a transparent estimator; enterprise volume routes to custom pricing.
