Straight answers

Before procurement asks.

What counts as an event?+

An event is a tracked user action such as a page view, checkout, or feature use. Duplicate and blocked events never count toward your allowance.

What happens if we exceed our volume?+

Nothing breaks. We notify admins at 80% and 100%, then use a predictable per-million overage rate. You can set a hard cap at any time.

Can we use our own warehouse?+

Yes. Scale connects to Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, and Databricks. Your warehouse can remain the source of truth.

Is annual pricing available?+

Annual plans include two months free and a locked event rate. Monthly billing remains available for Starter and Scale.
