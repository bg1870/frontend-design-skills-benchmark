GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Still deciding? Talk with our team for a recommendation.

What counts as a monthly event?

An event is a tracked user action, such as a signup, button click, or completed purchase. Duplicate and test events can be excluded.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrade at any time, or move to a lower tier at the end of your current billing period. Your historical data remains available within the new plan’s limits.

Do you offer startup or nonprofit pricing?

Eligible early-stage startups and registered nonprofits can receive discounted annual plans. Contact our team to check eligibility.

Is my product data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Advanced identity controls, audit logs, and regional hosting are available on Enterprise.
